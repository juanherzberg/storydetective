The blueprint

Every great college essay has a "what" and a "why"

Most students write about their "what" and stop there. The ones who get in go deeper. Colleges don't care what you do, but about why it matters to you or the world.

The what

Your interest, passion, or identity

Soccer, coding, origami, community, languages: whatever you care about.

The why

Why it matters to you and the world

The deeper truth: the value, trait, or drive that makes your story yours.

How it works

From what you think you want to say to what you really mean

Step 1

List your interests

Add anything that matters: sports, hobbies, identities, passions. No interest is too small.

Step 2

Answer reflective questions

For each interest, the AI asks questions to uncover the deeper meaning behind each "what".

Step 3: what you get

Your personalized essay roadmap

For each interest, you walk away with:

  • Why your story matters, the deeper meaning behind what you love.
  • The Common App prompt that fits your story.
  • Suggestion on how to open your essay.
  • The turning point that carries real weight and how it could arc.

A guide if you need it, validation if you don't.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does AI write my essay for me?

No, and that's the whole point. Story Detective never writes anything for you. It asks you reflective questions, helps you understand what your answers reveal, and identifies the deeper meaning behind your interests. You do all the writing. Your essay should sound like you, not like a machine.

How long does it take?

About 10 minutes. You answer a series of reflective questions about two of your interests and walk away with a personalized analysis and a recommended Common App prompt for each one.

Can't I just use ChatGPT for this?

You could, but ChatGPT will write your essay for you, and colleges can tell the difference. Story Detective does the opposite. It helps you find the story only you can tell, then gets out of the way so you can tell it yourself. The writing stays yours, and that's what makes it stand out.
